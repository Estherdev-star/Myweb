About us

Who we are

St. Esther Secondary School is a secondary school on the Mubende–Kampala Road, Mubende, Uganda. We educate students from Senior 1 to Senior 6 in a caring and orderly environment.

Our values

  • Be respectful
  • Be kind
  • Be safe
  • Be responsible

Mission

To give every learner a quality education and the character to use it well.

Vision

A school where every student reaches their full potential.

Subjects

Mathematics, English, sciences, humanities, languages and practical subjects.
