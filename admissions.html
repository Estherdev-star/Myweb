Admissions

Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Previous school report or PLE results
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Four passport photos
  • Parent or guardian contact details

How to apply

  • Visit the school or send us a message
  • Submit the documents above
  • Sit a short placement assessment
  • Receive your admission letter
